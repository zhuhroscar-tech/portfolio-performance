Cumulative realized return (% of contributed capital)

Methodology: a "closed trade" is one symbol's full round trip from zero shares back to zero shares, matched first-in-first-out. Total return is realized profit from all closed trades divided by total capital contributed to the account. Win rate is the share of closed trades with a positive result. Only percentages are published — no dollar amounts, account numbers, or symbol-level detail.

Scope: figures reflect only closed positions as of the date shown; currently open positions are excluded until they close. This is a personal record of realized performance, not an audited return series, and not a time-weighted or annualized return.

Data updates automatically when a new brokerage export is processed. Source and methodology: github.com/zhuhroscar-tech/portfolio-performance.
